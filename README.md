## what is this

Dhvani is a music player. But not the kind your phone ships with.

No ads. No subscription wall. No "upgrade to listen offline." No blurry album art. No 128kbps garbage.

Just your library. Playing back exactly how it was recorded. With a UI dark enough to disappear into.

I built this because every music app out there treats audio as a feature, not the point. Dhvani treats everything else as noise.

---

## what it does differently

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

**🎚️ 320kbps. Always.**  
Zero re-encoding, zero compression layers added on top of your files. If you put in lossless, you get lossless back.

**🚫 No Ads. Ever.**  
Not "skip in 5 seconds." Not "watch an ad for 30 minutes of offline." Zero. None. Nothing.

**🌑 True OLED Black**  
`#000000`. Not dark gray. Not "night mode." Pure black. Your AMOLED panel will thank you.

</td>
<td width="50%">

**📴 Offline, properly**  
Your entire library. Cached. No streaming, no buffering, no "content unavailable in your region." If it's on your phone, it plays.

**🎵 Playlists that make sense**  
No algorithm. No "we think you'll like this." Your playlists, built the way you want them.

**📱 A UI that doesn't fight you**  
The interface disappears. The music doesn't. That's it.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## the landing page

The website you might have seen is built with raw HTML + CSS + JS. No React. No framework. Just:

- **GSAP ScrollTrigger** — the scroll animations, scene transitions, the whole cinematic thing
- **Custom SVG filters** — the liquid glass effect, the chromatic aberration glitch
- **Canvas particles** — the ambient background dots that react to nothing and somehow still feel alive
- **Outfit + JetBrains Mono** — the only two fonts this project will ever need

No build step. No node_modules eating your disk. Open `index.html`. That's it.
